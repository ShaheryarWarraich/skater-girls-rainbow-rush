# 🛹 Skater Girls · Rainbow Rush 🌈

A 3D endless-runner game (Subway Surfers style) for kids — in a **single HTML file** that works fully offline.

**▶ Play it here: https://shaheryarwarraich.github.io/skater-girls-rainbow-rush/**

## Features
- **30 themed places** — Robot Boulevard, Bubblegum Lane, Neon Square (night!), Rainbow Sunset… all the way to Legend City
- **6 skater girls** — Muntaha & little Anya are free; unlock Zoya, Lily, Aisha and Luna with coins (magnet, shield & coin perks)
- **Evil robots** that look friendly until you get close — then jump over them!
- **Player profiles** — each player gets her own coins, unlocks and level progress
- **Touch + keyboard** — swipe ⬅➡⬆⬇ on tablets, or arrows / WASD / Space on a computer
- Distance-based levels that get faster and busier; crash and you keep your coins

## Tech
Everything is inside one `index.html`: Three.js (embedded, no internet needed), WebAudio-synthesized sound effects & music, localStorage saves. No build, no server — just open the file.

Made with ❤️ for Muntaha & Anya.
