How it works

Every morning {{ site.title }} pulls the RSS feeds of Pakistani newsrooms, groups articles that describe the same event, and produces one story per event rather than one per outlet. Each story is summarised in our own words — the publisher's own blurb is never reproduced here — and links directly to the reporting it is based on, both the lead outlet and any others that also covered it.

Stories are scored and ranked by an editorial model where available. When that model is unavailable or fails, the edition falls back to a transparent, rule-based ranking instead, and the page for that day says so.

What this site is not

{{ site.title }} does not run any JavaScript, load any external fonts or scripts, or track visitors in any way. It does not republish full articles, and it does not claim any reporting as its own — every story links back to the outlets that did the work.
